# **📄 Business Case: E‑Commerce Orders & Customer Insights Dashboard**

Modern e‑commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional and customer data, but decision‑makers often struggle to translate this information into clear insights that support growth, retention, and operational efficiency. This project addresses that challenge by building a streamlined analytics solution using Power BI, based on two core datasets: **Orders** and **Customers**.

The objective is to provide a **single source of truth** for understanding customer behaviour, revenue performance, and order trends. By integrating both datasets into a clean relational model, the dashboard enables stakeholders to answer critical business questions such as:

- Which regions generate the highest revenue and order volume  
- How customer sign‑ups influence sales performance  
- What the Average Order Value (AOV) is and how it changes over time  
- Which customers contribute most to revenue  
- How order activity trends across months and seasons  

The dashboard centralises key performance indicators (KPIs) — including Total Revenue, Total Orders, Customer Count, and AOV — allowing managers to quickly assess business health. Supporting visuals such as revenue trends, regional performance, and customer segmentation provide deeper insight into operational patterns and growth opportunities.

This solution demonstrates how a simple, well‑structured dataset can be transformed into a **decision‑ready analytics product**, showcasing strong skills in data modelling, DAX, data cleaning, and dashboard design. It serves as a practical example of how Power BI can drive data‑driven decision‑making in a real‑world e‑commerce context.
